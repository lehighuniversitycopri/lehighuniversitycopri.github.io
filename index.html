Coasts, Oceans, Ports & Rivers at Lehigh

The Lehigh University student chapter of ASCE’s Coasts, Oceans, Ports & Rivers Institute — a home for graduate students working on coastal resilience, water infrastructure, climate adaptation and the rivers in our own backyard.

Join the chapter See events

About us

We connect students tackling today’s most pressing water challenges. Through speakers, site visits, research roundtables and social events, we help members share their work, build professional connections and grow as engineers — while engaging with the Lehigh and Delaware River communities around us.

Coasts

Shorelines, storm surge and resilient coastal communities.

Oceans

Waves, offshore structures and the open sea.

Ports

Harbors, navigation and the infrastructure that moves goods.

Rivers

Hydraulics, sediment, flooding and water resources.

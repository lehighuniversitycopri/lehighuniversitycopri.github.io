About us

We bring together students in coastal engineering, the field that protects shorelines, harbors and coastal communities from waves, storm surge, erosion and rising seas. Our members study everything from wave mechanics and sediment transport to resilient ports and nature-based shoreline protection. Through speakers, site visits, research roundtables and social events, we help them share their work, build professional connections and grow as engineers.

About COPRI

The Coasts, Oceans, Ports & Rivers Institute was founded in 2000 as a semi-autonomous institute of the American Society of Civil Engineers, the oldest national engineering society in the United States. It works to advance coastal, ocean, port, waterway, river and wetland engineering for the good of society, and welcomes allied professionals from outside engineering as well.

Membership activities

As a member, you can take part in everything the chapter does:

  • Guest speakers

    Talks from leaders in industry, academia and government.

  • Site visits

    Trips to ports, waterways and critical water infrastructure projects.

  • Research roundtables

    Share work in progress and learn from your peers.

  • Conferences

    Take part in COPRI specialty conferences and professional events.

  • Mentorship

    Students at every level learning from each other and from professionals in the field.

  • Community engagement

    Projects focused on the Lehigh and Delaware River regions.

  • Socials and networking

    Events that turn classmates and colleagues into a real network.
